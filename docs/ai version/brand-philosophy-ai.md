# Brand Philosophy (AI Version)

Target: Brand identity logic, meaning, symbolism. Zero fluff.

## 1. Name: Ember
Ember = glowing remnant of fire. Hold heat after flame die, rekindle fire later.
Symbolic role: Preserve critical project context across time. Knowledge survive member departure. Origin for character: Lantern Keeper born from ember, carry eternal ember.

## 2. Logo
Visual: Dark fractured shell, glowing internal core.
Meaning: Durable knowledge preserved beneath surface noise. Core stay alive while project change.
Connection: Glow link directly to lantern ember in character lore.

## 3. Character: Lantern Keeper
Identity: Small hooded traveler born from ember. Wander unknown land. Meet travelers, observe path, record decisions, findings, lessons.

Evolution:
- Early: Record events in grimoire without grand goal.
- Realization: Past lessons guide future travelers. Previous finding = new traveler baseline.
- Core purpose: Carry learned knowledge forward. Prevent restart from scratch.

Artifacts:
- Lantern + Eternal Ember: Never-dying core knowledge.
- Grimoire: Plain record of observations, facts, choices.
- Staff: Guidance, forward direction.
- Hood: Humility facing unknown.
Role: Custodian and witness only. Not owner, not final authority. Knowledge open to question, correction, extension.
Central rule: What project learn must not die with learner. Next builders resume from ember, not start from zero.
