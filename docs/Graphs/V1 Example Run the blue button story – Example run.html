V1 example run: the blue button story

One small story, scene by scene. Each scene shows what happened, what Ember does, and what Ember holds afterwards.

  • AI suggests
  • Automatic check (no AI)
  • You decide
  • Saved

This story is an illustration. A real AI may suggest different items or links. The steps for closing, flagging, reading, and resetting were replayed with the real commands on 2026-10-30, and the results match.

Scene 1: the first notes

The team chats after testing the app. Both lines are worth keeping.

“Blue buttons failed the contrast test.”
“We decided: dark green buttons.”

  1. AI suggests

    Capture: two items

    The AI suggests a Finding and a Decision.

  2. Automatic check

    Capture: checks

    The types are allowed and the quotes appear in the source.

  3. You decide

    Capture: you confirm

    You say yes to both.

    Command: ember capture

  4. AI suggests

    Relate: one link

    The AI suggests that the Decision depends on the Finding. The automatic check confirms this kind of link is allowed here.

  5. You decide

    Relate: you confirm

    You say yes.

    Command: ember relate --latest

Ember now holds

  • Finding · Active · newBlue buttons failed the contrast test
  • Decision · Active · newUse dark green buttons
  • Link: the Decision depends on the Finding

A new test says dark green passes. It answers the Question and backs up the Decision.

  1. AI suggests

    Capture: one item

    The AI suggests a new Finding.

  2. You decide

    Capture: you confirm

  3. AI suggests

    Relate: two links

    The AI suggests that the Finding answers the Question and supports the Decision.

  4. You decide

Scene 4: a surprise

Later the team finds that the first test was set up wrong. The blue result cannot be trusted.

  1. You decide

    You close the first Finding

    You close it as invalidated (found to be wrong).

    Command: ember close-item --item 1 --reason invalidated

  2. Automatic check

    Maintain: the knock-on rule

    The Decision depends on that Finding. Rule 4 marks the Decision as Challenged.

Ember now holds

  • Finding · Closed: invalidatedBlue buttons failed the contrast test
  • Decision · ChallengedUse dark green buttons
  • Question · Closed: answeredDoes dark green pass the test?
  • Finding · ActiveDark green passes the new test

Scene 5: reading it back

Someone asks:

“Why did we pick green?”

  1. Automatic check

    Query: the reasons

    Ember shows the Decision, marked Challenged, with the two items it rests on: the closed Finding and the new Finding that supports it.

    Commands: ember current-state, ember why --item 2

  2. You decide

    You recheck

    The new Finding still backs the Decision up. You set the Decision back to Active.

    Command: ember set-status --item 2 --to Active

Ember now holds

  • Finding · Closed: invalidatedBlue buttons failed the contrast test
  • Decision · Active · set by youUse dark green buttons
  • Question · Closed: answeredDoes dark green pass the test?
  • Finding · ActiveDark green passes the new test
