Purpose: This document explains the philosophy and meaning behind Ember's brand identity. It captures the reasoning behind the name, the symbolism of the logo, and the mythology and symbolism of its character, the Lantern Keeper. Use it as a reference when creating, evaluating, or evolving Ember's visual identity and brand communication.


## 1. The Name Ember

An ember is a small, glowing remnant of a fire. It retains warmth after the flames recede and holds the potential to rekindle them.

We chose the name to represent Ember's purpose: preserving essential context so that knowledge, continuity, and progress endure over time. The ember also gives the character its origin: the Lantern Keeper is born from an ember and carries an eternal ember on its journey.

## 2. The Logo

A dark, fractured exterior surrounds a glowing core that remains alive within.

It represents essential knowledge preserved beneath the surface, remaining available and relevant even as a project evolves. The contrast between the dark exterior and the inner glow embodies the idea of keeping what matters alive.

The glowing core echoes the eternal ember carried by the Lantern Keeper, connecting the logo to the character and the wider brand mythology.

## 3. The Character — The Lantern Keeper

The Lantern Keeper is a small, hooded wanderer born from an ember. It begins its existence without a destination or an understanding of its purpose, and sets out through unfamiliar lands. Along the way, it encounters travelers and witnesses their journeys: their experiences, discoveries, decisions, and lessons.

At first, the Keeper simply records what it witnesses and learns in an ordinary grimoire. Over time, it discovers that these records can help others who come later. A lesson from one journey can guide another; a discovery can become the starting point for someone else's work. Through these encounters, the Keeper discovers its purpose: to carry what has been learned forward, so those who follow can build upon what came before.

The Keeper carries a lantern containing a single eternal ember that never dies. It also carries an ordinary grimoire in which it records everything it witnesses and learns. Its staff represents guidance and direction, while its hood reflects humility before the unknown. These are the tools of a traveler and record-keeper, not symbols of authority.

The Keeper is a witness and custodian of knowledge, not its owner or final authority. What it records can guide others, but it does not prevent them from questioning, correcting, or extending what came before. Its journey has no fixed destination; its purpose is discovered through the journeys of others.

The character embodies Ember's central idea: what is learned should not disappear with the person who learned it. Preserving that knowledge gives those who follow a place to continue from, rather than forcing them to begin again.
